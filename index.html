About Me

Senior UX Researcher

I'm Mason Kelly, a Senior UX Researcher at CBRE leading a team of 4 researchers across 15 modules on a $30M enterprise platform serving 100K+ users. I specialize in quantitative UX measurement and mixed-methods research, managing longitudinal metrics programs, running statistical analyses in Python, SQL, and R, and grounding qualitative insights in data to drive measurable product outcomes.

I hold an MS in Business Analytics from the University of Virginia's Darden School of Business and a BA in Sociology from James Madison University. This combination of advanced analytics training and social science gives me a unique ability to translate complex user behavior data into actionable product strategy, connecting UX metrics directly to business KPIs and product roadmap decisions.
