About Me.

Senior UX Researcher

I'm Mason Kelly, a Senior UX Researcher at CBRE. I lead a team of 4 UX Researchers across 15 modules on a $30M enterprise platform serving over 100K users. My work focuses on driving product strategy through mixed-methods research -- combining qualitative insights from interviews, usability testing, and journey mapping with quantitative analysis using tools like Python, SQL, and advanced statistical methods.

I graduated from James Madison University with a BA in Sociology and from the University of Virginia's Darden School of Business with an MS in Business Analytics. This unique combination of social science and analytics training allows me to bridge the gap between deep user empathy and data-driven decision-making, delivering research that directly impacts product roadmaps and business outcomes.
